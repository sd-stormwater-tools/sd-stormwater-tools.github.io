Independent plan review toolkit

San Diego Storm Water
Reviewer Tools

Reference tools for reviewing storm water submittals for projects in San Diego. Each tool links back to the public source data and the manual or code sections it relies on, so results can be checked against the governing documents.

Not an official City of San Diego product. Preliminary reference tools - verify with source documents.

Key references

  • City of San Diego Land Development Manual Storm Water Standards (Part 1 BMP Design Manual, Aug 2024 Ed.), Drainage Design Manual, and Appendix O GIS files (ESA and ASBS shapefiles, SWQMP templates, worksheets).
  • San Diego Municipal Code Including Ch. 4, Art. 3, Div. 3 (Stormwater Management and Discharge Control) and Ch. 14, Art. 2, Div. 2 (Storm Water Runoff and Drainage Regulations).
  • County of San Diego 85th Percentile Isopluvials (HYD_85TH_PERCENT_ISOPLUVIALS) Public County DPW map service for the 85th-percentile 24-hour storm depth lines (see Figure B.1-1, Storm Water Standards Part 1).
