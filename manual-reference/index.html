About & sources

    
Not an official City of San Diego product. Independent reviewer reference tool. It does not replace the governing documents; confirm the current edition on sandiego.gov before relying on any page.

    
Reference hierarchy used for ordering results: (1) San Diego Municipal Code, (2) Stormwater Standards Manual, (3) Drainage Design Manual.

    

    
Page numbers are PDF page numbers of the hosted copies, which are byte-identical to the City files listed above (MD5 shown). Printed page labels are read from each page's footer. Thumbnails are pre-rendered from the same files. The Stormwater Standards file bundles Part 1 (August 2024 Edition) with Parts 2 and 3, which still carry their May 2021 Edition footers. The City's separate January 2018 sizing/feasibility worksheets PDF is not used because it predates the August 2024 revisions to Worksheets B.5-2 and B.5-4; use Appendix B of the manual or the City's 2024 Excel worksheets.
